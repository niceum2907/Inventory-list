Settings

Shared data

Connecting…

Stock, usage and deliveries are stored in one shared database. Every change appears on all devices and for every colleague who opens this link, within seconds. Photo recognition and email reading use your Claude account – no API key needed.

Backup

Loading replaces all current data. A backup of the current data is downloaded first. Requires the admin password.

Save a backup now and then. To move data from the old local version: open the old file, Settings → Save backup, then load that file here.

Use on your phone

  • Open this app’s claude.ai link on your phone and sign in with your Skyhawk Claude account.
  • iPhone (Safari): tap the Share button, then “Add to Home Screen”.
  • Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu, then “Add to Home screen”.
  • Colleagues: share the link with them (Share → Contributor) so they can update the stock too.

Delete all stock

Removes every item from the stock list. This cannot be undone. Requires the admin password.
